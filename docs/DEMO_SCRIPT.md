# Demo video script (≈ 1:45)

**Setup before recording**
- `npm run seed && npm start`
- Window 1 (desktop): organizer dashboard `http://localhost:3000/o/DEMO24#key=demo-organizer-key`
- Window 2 (phone, or browser devtools at 375×812): `http://<LAN-IP>:3000/e/DEMO24`
- Optional window 3: kiosk `http://localhost:3000/k/DEMO24`

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Kiosk screen with big QR and live headcount | "At every mixer you walk up to a stranger with no idea who they are. Beacon fixes that." |
| 0:08 | Phone scans QR, fills profile: photo, *looking for: fundraising, mentorship*, *spot me: white shirt, black backpack* | "Scan the QR at the door and fill in a 45-second profile: what you're looking for, what you can help with, and how people can spot you." |
| 0:22 | *For you* tab: ranked matches with % and reasons | "Beacon ranks the people in the room worth meeting and tells you why. This investor can help with fundraising, and we share a niche interest." |

| 0:48 | Tap **Wave**; toast "You're connected"; contact details appear | "Send a wave. Once they wave back you're connected and contact details unlock, never before." |
| 0:58 | Tap **Find each other** and the phone flashes full-screen colour | "Can't find them in the crowd? Both phones light up in your colour so you can spot each other." |
| 1:06 | *My badge* QR + *Here now* live list updating | "Met someone by chance? Scan their badge to swap contacts. The live list shows who's here right now." |
| 1:14 | Dashboard: press **Simulate live activity**; stats tick, chart and feed move | "Organizers get a live radar: who's here, who's left, and how many connections are being made." |
