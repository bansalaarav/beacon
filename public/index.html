How Beacon solves the “who is that?” problem

🟢 Live presence

Real-time list of who's here, who just arrived and who left. Nobody has to refresh, and people who close the app get checked out automatically.

✦ Explainable matches

Pairs your needs with other people's offers, weights rare shared interests higher, and says why each person is worth meeting.

🧥 Spot me

Every profile has a photo, a colour and a line like “red hoodie, near the bar”, so you can actually find the person.

🔆 Beacon flash

Can't find them in the crowd? Both phones flash the same colour so you can wave each other down.

💬 AI icebreakers

Claude writes an opening line from both profiles. It falls back to templates when offline.

🪪 Scan to connect

Scan someone's badge QR or type their 4-digit code to swap contacts instantly. Export everyone as vCards afterwards.

🔀 Mixer rounds

Organizers make well-matched small groups with one tap. Each person gets a table and a topic on their phone.

📊 Organizer radar

Live attendance, zone heatmap, supply and demand gaps, people nobody has talked to yet, and one-tap intros.
